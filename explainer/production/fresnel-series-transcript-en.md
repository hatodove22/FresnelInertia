Scope and limits
The WHC2025Demo CAD is used to illustrate the series. Its name does not prove exact identity with the photographed July 2025 apparatus; earlier prototypes and electronics layouts differ.
The historical design has three contact planes for an illustrated thumb/index/middle grip. The CAD channel labels A/B/C identify geometry; finger assignment is editorial, consistent with that grip, not independently proven by CAD.
Contact angles, input waveforms, colors and virtual-object graphics are illustrative. The film's chosen angle range is not a measurement or claim about hardware travel.
Grip force and acceleration are illustrated inputs, not live telemetry. No hardware was actuated for this film.
The CHI 2025 method and statistical distinction were checked in a locally held author manuscript. The final ACM PDF was inaccessible. The manuscript reports a significant coherence effect and only a nonsignificant softness trend; the film preserves that distinction.
The WHC/VRSJ center-of-gravity and inertia work provides demonstration and model evidence, not a perceptual equivalent-mass validation. Figure directions and gains are not calibrated CAD or firmware motor-sign commands.
Local contact orientation does not mechanically reproduce whole-object shape, fingertip spacing, bulk compliance, or unrestricted forces. No four-vibration-channel effects are attributed to this historical mechanism.
